# Hackathon Submission Response

## 1. What real-world problem are you solving?

Students waste 5-10 hours weekly on ineffective study practices, without focusing on their weak areas or following a structured approach. Traditional one-size-fits-all study methods don't account for individual learning patterns, leading to suboptimal exam performance despite significant time investment. This affects millions of students preparing for board exams, competitive tests, or professional certifications who struggle with inefficient study practices.

## 2. Who is this problem for?

- High school students preparing for board exams
- College students studying for competitive exams (JEE, NEET, GRE, etc.)
- Professionals preparing for certification exams
- Self-learners seeking efficient study methods
- Any student who feels overwhelmed by their study material and doesn't know where to start

## 3. How does your solution use AI?

The solution uses Google Gemini 1.5 Flash to:
1. Analyze student profiles including syllabi, performance history, and time constraints to create customized study schedules (Personalized Study Plan Generator)
2. Generate adaptive practice questions tailored to each student's weak areas and current proficiency level (Adaptive Question Generator)
3. Provide progress insights and adjust recommendations based on performance trends (Progress Tracker & Wellness Advisor)
4. Incorporate cognitive science principles to prevent burnout and maximize retention (Wellness Advisor)

The AI layer is load-bearing - removing it would break the solution since manual creation of personalized study plans at scale would be impossible. The system transforms complex educational data into actionable, individualized learning strategies that save students significant time while improving outcomes.
