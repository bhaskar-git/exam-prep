What This Hackathon Is About:
This is a Hackathon with limited time (ends on 5th May, 11:59 pm (IST)), self‑paced hackathon designed to help you think and build like an AI Generalist. 
An AI Generalist is someone who can: 
● Identify a real problem in their work or industry 
● Decide where AI adds leverage (and where it doesn’t) 
● Design a simple but effective AI system (prompts, workflows, tools) 
● Ship an end-to-end usable solution by leveraging any of the AI tools you have learned with us so far This is not about flashy demos or complex tech. It’s about clear thinking, practical impact, and smart use of AI. How the Hackathon Works 
● Submission deadline: 5th May, 11:59 pm (IST) 
● You will think about and pick a real-world idea and build on your own during the week 
● You submit your project at the end of 10 days The intro call explains the format. The real work happens after. What You Need to Build 
● A working AI‑powered solution. You can leverage tools like N8N, Emergent, Gemini Studio, make.com, etc. 
● The solution you build should leverage AI, and the problem statement should be very realistic and real-world. Evaluation Criteria (100 Points)
- Problem Framing – 25 pts 
Is the problem real, specific, and relevant to professionals/students/businesses/freelancers (can be a problem from any profession or domain)?
- AI Leverage & System Design – 30 pts 
Is AI used thoughtfully? Does it add real leverage?
- Practical Usefulness – 20 pts 
Does this save time, reduce effort, or improve outcomes?
- Execution Quality – 15 pts 
Does it work end‑to‑end? Is it usable? (You need to submit a video/screen recording that shows that your solution is working end-to-end.)
- Clarity of Explanation – 10 pts 
Can you clearly explain what you built and why it matters? We reward clarity and usefulness, not complexity.

Three pieces. Don't miss one.
Drive folder + Google Form + LinkedIn post. Each has a specific shape. Do them in this order on submission day.
Piece 1 — The Drive folder
Open your Google Drive. Create one parent folder named "Hackathon Submission" or "[Your project name] — Hackathon Submission". Inside, create exactly four sub-folders. Click the folder below to see the structure.
📁 Hackathon Submission / [your project name]›
⚠ #1 reason submissions get disqualified
Share your folder correctly. Right-click → Share → Share. Two things must happen: (1) Add product@houseofedtech.in as Editor. (2) Set General Access to "Anyone with the link · Viewer." Do both. Not one. Both.

07 · Hacks & Tips
Small tricks that save whole days.
The n8n dot hack
Extend your n8n 14-day trial indefinitely using a quirk of Gmail. Gmail ignores dots in addresses — yourname@gmail.com and your.name@gmail.com reach the same inbox. But n8n treats them as different users. OTPs still land in your original inbox. Click below to see examples.
Originalnandiniraj@gmail.com
Trial 2nandini.raj@gmail.com
Trial 3nandi.ni.raj@gmail.com
Trial 4n.andini.r.aj@gmail.com
Export your workflows as JSON before a trial ends — so you can re-import them into the new account and not lose your work.
Start ugly, finish polished
Ship a working version by day 2. No styling. No polish. Just the core loop — input → AI step → output. Once that works, you have 8 days to make it pretty. If you spend day 1 on CSS, you'll discover on day 5 that your AI doesn't actually do what you thought, and now you're out of time.
The principle
Working-ugly beats beautiful-broken. Every single time.
Use ChatGPT as your co-pilot the whole 10 days
Keep it open in a tab. Every friction you hit, ask it first.
•	→Stuck on an idea? "Give me 10 hackathon ideas in finance."
•	→Prompt not working? "Improve this prompt: [paste]"
•	→Video script? "Write a 7-min demo-video script for this project."
•	→LinkedIn post? "Draft a LinkedIn post announcing this project."
•	→Stuck on n8n? "How do I connect X to Y in n8n?"
One rehearsal before recording
Before you hit record on your final video, do a full run-through out loud without recording. You'll find two or three things you forgot to prepare. Fix them. Then record. This one rehearsal saves you from three failed takes.
08 · FAQ
Common questions, answered once.
Will the deadline be extended?
›
Extremely unlikely. 5 May midnight is firm. Plan as if it will not move.
Can I team up with someone?
›
No. Individual only. Group hackathons may come in future rounds.
Can I submit multiple projects?
›
Yes, unlimited. Each one needs its own Drive folder and its own form submission.
Do I need to register beforehand?
›
No. Submission equals registration. One form, at the end. Nothing else.
Can I use a tool be10x didn't teach?
›
Absolutely. Any tool, taught or not. The only rule is that AI must play a meaningful role in your solution.
Can I reuse an old project?
›
Yes — adapt it, extend it, add the AI layer where it was missing. Be honest in your write-up about what's new and what AI is adding. Don't submit last year's project unchanged.
What if my idea is already a commercial product?
›
Totally fine. You're not judged on novelty — you're judged on whether YOU built it, whether it works, and whether it solves a real problem.
Can I build a game or an ML model instead of an app?
›
Games: yes, if the game solves a real-world problem (e.g. education, habit-forming). Pure entertainment games are harder to justify. ML models: yes, as long as it's tied to a real use case and your video clearly shows the input-to-output pipeline working on real data.
Are mentors available?
›
No dedicated mentors. Use LMS support, your trainers in existing sessions, and LinkedIn to ask the hosts directly.
Will there be a certificate?
›
Team is finalizing format. Likely a participation e-certificate, with something additional for winners.
When are results announced?
›
Judges start evaluating from Wednesday 6 May. Results realistically come the week of 11 May. Winners are contacted directly via LMS / email.
What language for the video?
›
English or Hindi only. Judges come from varied regions; other languages can't be guaranteed coverage.
